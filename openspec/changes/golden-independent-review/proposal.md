## Why

The demo serves the versioned golden answers in evaluation mode, without a model: task-api Q1–Q3
(`seeds/cache/task-api.json`, DIS-32) and acme-shop Q1, Q3, Q4 (`seeds/cache/acme-shop.json`,
regenerated in DIS-32 decision E7, which reset the reviews DIS-104 had left). All six have
`reviews: []`: nobody has checked them against the fixture code. On 2026-10-27 the author changed
the procedure: the review is no longer her work but that of **independent agents of a model
different from the generator** (the golden answers were generated by `qwen2.5-coder`, a local 7B
model). This change (Linear DIS-33, CM-HU-18.4, parent DIS-20 / CM-HU-18) has that review done by
a blind panel of two agents (Opus and Sonnet) under a criterion written before any judgement, so
that every golden the demo serves carries a traceable verdict with `file:line` of the fixture and
the evaluator can trust it without any review by the author.

## What Changes

- **Criterion first.** `docs/golden-review/2026-10-task-api-golden-review.md` starts with
  `## Criterion` (what `correct`, `partial`, `incorrect` and an error are) and `## Panel` (models,
  allowed and forbidden sources), committed before the panel input exists.
- **Blind panel input.** `docs/golden-review/panel/2026-10-golden-review-input.json`: the six golden
  answers projected to question, answer, statements (text, type, the evidence each one cites) and
  evidence locations (path, lines), without excerpts, verification marks, confidence, fingerprints
  or ids; plus the behaviour item task-api Q4 (`UNKNOWN (no-anchor)`, no golden answer). Seven
  items.
- **Panel (outside the apply).** Panelist 1 (Opus) and panelist 2 (Sonnet) judge the seven items
  blind to each other, from the criterion, the input and the fixture code only; a resolver (Opus,
  fresh context) decides only the items where their verdicts differ. Outputs in
  `docs/golden-review/panel/` (`…-panelist-1.json`, `…-panelist-2.json`, `…-resolution.json`).
- **Signatures.** Every golden whose final verdict is `correct` or `partial` gets in its `reviews`
  the two panelists' signatures `{"by": "agent:<model>", "date": "<panel day>"}`; never the
  author's. The golden format does not change.
- **Incorrect answers.** A golden whose final verdict is `incorrect` is left unsigned, named in the
  report and handed to a successor ticket that regenerates (or retires) it and has it reviewed again
  (design D5).
- **Report.** The rest of the report: one row per item (each panelist's verdict, the final one, the
  justification with `file:line`, the errors), the agreement (percent and Cohen's kappa over the
  seven verdicts, kappa indicative) and the limitations.
- **Coherence test.** `tests/unit/seed/golden-reviews.spec.ts` ties the golden files, the panel
  files and the report together (input projection, output shape and citations, resolution =
  disagreements, signatures, recomputed agreement).
- **Docs.** `fixtures/README.md` (who reviewed the golden answers and where the report is),
  `docs/project-context.md` (no more "the author signs a sample"), `readme.md` (the
  `golden-review/` tree comment), `docs/DEMO.md` (reviewed by a panel of agents), and the TSDoc of
  `reviews` in `packages/cli/src/cache/golden-file.ts`.

Out of scope (non-goals of DIS-33): generating or regenerating golden answers (DIS-32; an incorrect
one goes to a successor ticket); changing the golden format (`verdict` in `reviews`) or the
behaviour of `cache:build` and `verify`; freezing the panel outputs with SHA-256 (no later
measurement consumes them, unlike DIS-80); reviewing live answers or Table 1 (DIS-82).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `golden-answers`: the versioned golden answers gain an independent review: a blind panel input,
  validated panel outputs, the panel's signatures in `reviews` and a review report whose figures
  are recomputed by a test.

## Impact

- Data: `seeds/cache/task-api.json` and `seeds/cache/acme-shop.json` (only `reviews`, canonical
  render kept); new `docs/golden-review/2026-10-task-api-golden-review.md` and
  `docs/golden-review/panel/*.json`.
- Code: no production behaviour changes; only the TSDoc of `GoldenReview` / `reviews` in
  `packages/cli/src/cache/golden-file.ts`. `git diff --stat final-project-CRN -- packages/core` stays
  empty. No new dependency; the test reuses `cohenKappa` of `scripts/annotation-agreement.mjs`.
- Tests: new `tests/unit/seed/golden-reviews.spec.ts`; no existing test changes.
- `npm run verify` keeps printing `acme-shop: 3/3 …` and `task-api: 3/3 …` (`verify` does not read
  `reviews`; `seeds/cache/` is no fingerprint input).
- No personal data, no network, no model call from code: the panel agents are run by the autonomous
  workflow. The panel input copies no `excerpt`, so the planted secret of
  `fixtures/task-api/src/config/env.ts` does not leave the fixture.
- Commands and constraints: `docs/project-context.md`, `.claude/sdd-harness.env`.
