## 2. Requirements missing or partial

None found. Two points checked closely:

- "The merge SHALL fail, writing nothing, when ... the resolution answers an agreed answer": through the CLI the
  resolution is validated first against the disagreements (`scripts/answer-review.mjs:519`), so an extra answer is
  rejected as `unknown answer acme-shop Q01` (exit 1, no file). The requirement asks that the project and question be
  named, which they are; the clearer message of `mergeVerdicts` (l.375) is reached only by direct calls. Recorded as a
  Minor in the adversarial review, not as a gap.
- "Every `sourcesRead` entry SHALL be ... one file": an empty `sourcesRead` is accepted. The spec states no minimum,
  and the report's limitations say the list is self-declared.
